2. **Content slide:** The main point
3. **CTA slide:** "Link in bio" or "Swipe up"

## Engagement Tips

### Daily Tasks (5 minutes)
1. Reply to comments on your posts
2. Like/comment on 5-10 posts from target athletes
3. Check DMs and respond

### Weekly Tasks (30 minutes)
1. Find 10 new HYROX/running athletes to follow
2. Comment meaningfully on their training posts
3. Share user content to stories (with credit)

### Finding Content
1. Search #HYROX, #HYROXTraining on Instagram
2. Look at Strava leaderboards for active athletes
3. Check Facebook groups: "HYROX Athletes", "Marathon Training"
4. Monitor Twitter for fitness influencers

## Content Ideas for Different Situations

### When You Have No User Content Yet
- Share motivational quotes about training
- Post educational content about "What is Proof of Sweat?"
- Behind-the-scenes of building Athlyst
- Personal training posts from your own account
- Industry news/trends about fitness + crypto

### When Athletes Start Posting
- Screenshot and share their workouts (with permission)
- Create highlight reels of top performers
- Interview athletes via DM for spotlight posts
- Share their milestones (first workout, streak achievements)

### When You Have Momentum
- Weekly/monthly recaps with data
- Athlete success stories
- Community challenges
- User-generated content campaigns
