# Athlyst Context (source of truth)

## What Athlyst is
Athlyst.fun is an Instagram-first, pre-launch community (“Founding Athlysts”) for people who treat training as identity—not just a hobby. The product direction is to make athletic effort *legible* and *shareable*: a profile layer that can reflect proof-of-work from training (e.g., workout logs, consistency streaks, performance milestones), and turn that into status, community access, and motivation.

Planned product ideas (directional, not promised):
- “Proof-of-Sweat” style verification via training data sources (e.g., Strava/Garmin-style logs)
- Athlete “aura” / reputation scores driven by consistency + events + effort signals
- Community + gated access (inner circles, challenges, recognition loops)
- A modern identity layer for hybrid fitness (HYROX-style) and endurance athletes

**What Athlyst is NOT (for public comms right now):**
- Not an investment product, not “get rich,” not token speculation
- No performance promises (“this will make you faster/leaner”)
- No medical/health claims

## ICP hypothesis
### Primary ICP: Hybrid / HYROX-style athletes + Strava-heavy trainers
Observable traits:
- Trains 4–6x/week, cares about measurable progress
- Posts workouts, events, or training blocks (or at least consumes that content)
- Drawn to discipline/status/identity signaling: “earned, not claimed”
- Likes challenges, streaks, leaderboards, or structured programs
- possibly drawn to crypto 

Pain / desire:
- Effort feels invisible outside a small circle
- Wants recognition/community with people who “get it”
- Wants motivation loops beyond generic fitness content

Where they hang out:
- Instagram: HYROX athletes, hybrid fitness pages, coaches, race/event accounts
- Strava culture pages, running/hybrid communities
- Niche fitness creators (small/mid-size accounts with engaged comment sections)

### Secondary ICP: Crypto-native “fitness degens” + builders
Observable traits:
- Likes internet-native identity, gamification, composable profiles
- Comfortable with wallets/points systems (but we do NOT frame as investing)
- Active on X / Farcaster; early adopters

Use-case hook:
- “Identity + effort proof” as a digital primitive (status + access), without hype.

## Tone
- Direct, grounded, “earned-not-given”
- Slightly intense / serious: discipline, craft, consistency
- Zero cringe, zero hype, no grand promises
- Speak like a coach + founder: simple, specific, actionable
- Avoid: “moon,” “alpha,” “investment,” “token pump,” “100x,” or anything implying financial returns

## Channels
### Instagram (primary)
Goal: drive replies → DMs → waitlist conversions.
Tools:
- Comments on target accounts (fast, specific, value-add)
- Story interactions (polls/questions) to start conversations
- Permission-based DMs (no spam)
- Pinned post + highlights: “Founding Athlysts”

### X + Farcaster (secondary)
Goal: attract early adopters/builders + seed narrative.
Use:
- Short “earned identity” threads
- Build-in-public snippets
- Cross-post key milestones, but keep IG as the conversion engine

## Constraints (non-negotiables)
- Privacy-first: only collect email for waitlist; any future training-data integration must be opt-in with clear consent.
- No misleading claims: don’t imply partnerships, integrations, metrics, or launch dates unless true.
- No investment framing: do not market anything as a financial opportunity.
- No health/medical promises. No body-shaming.
- Keep scope realistic: daily plan must fit 60–90 minutes/day.
